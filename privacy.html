Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 30, 2025

At Percentage Calculator Pro, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website.

Information We Collect

Our calculator is designed to respect your privacy. Here's what you should know about the data we collect:

Personal Information

We do not collect any personally identifiable information when you use our calculator tools. All calculations are performed directly in your browser and are not transmitted to our servers.

Contact Form Information

If you use our contact form, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, email address, and message content. This information is used solely to respond to your inquiries.

Usage Data

We collect anonymous usage data, such as browser type, device information, and pages visited, to improve our service and user experience. This data cannot be used to identify individual users.

How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our service
  • To respond to your inquiries or feedback
  • To improve our website and user experience
  • To monitor and analyze usage patterns and trends

Cookies

We use cookies to store your preferences, such as dark mode settings. These cookies are essential for the functionality of our website and do not contain any personal information.

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services, such as analytics providers, to help us understand how our website is used. These third parties may collect information about your use of our website, but are bound by their own privacy policies.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to access and receive a copy of your personal information
  • The right to rectify or update your personal information
  • The right to erase your personal information
  • The right to restrict processing of your personal information
  • The right to object to processing of your personal information
  • The right to data portability

Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

Contact Us
